# Service warranty and repeated problems

Providers offer no warranty or 1-365 days with explicit coverage/exclusions in quotes. The form offers common durations. Inspection-only offers carry no repair warranty. Repair revisions state replacement warranty terms; drivers see them before approval. The last approved repair revision, or original selected quote when no revision exists, supplies the agreed terms. These source documents cannot be changed after acceptance. Older offers have no recorded warranty, rather than an invented promise.

The invoice shows terms and expiry from driver-confirmed completion. Same problem again opens the existing service report with photo evidence required. Reports can still be submitted outside the period: eligibility is reviewed against the actual terms, not automatically accepted or denied. Providers can offer a free recheck or explain why coverage does not apply. Extra paid follow-up requires a new request and approved quote; the completed invoice cannot be increased.

Admin Complaints lists these reports through the existing live disputes query. Admins view photos, replies, warranty and invoice history, assign priority/deadline and publish audited decisions. Decisions do not perform refunds. Provider dashboard recent-job report cards update through Firestore streams; no background push/email is promised.

Current limitation: one service complaint per job. A job with an existing complaint opens that case rather than creating a second warranty case. Closed cases need admin follow-up/review; there is no automatic repeat visit dispatch or appointment booking.
